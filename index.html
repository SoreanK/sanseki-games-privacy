Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our Apps. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

Automatically Collected Information

Our Apps use Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect certain non-personal information, including:

This information is collected and processed by Google AdMob in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not store, transmit, or process this data ourselves.

Our Apps are distributed through the Google Play Store and use Google Play Services, which may collect diagnostic and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Data Storage

Game progress (level completion, stars, settings preferences) is stored locally on your device using device storage (SharedPreferences / Capacitor Preferences). This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Our Apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.
